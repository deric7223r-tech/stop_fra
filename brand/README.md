# Guarding Assessment Brand

This directory contains brand assets and guidelines for **Guarding Assessment**, the marketing agency behind Stop FRA.

## Brand Overview

**Guarding Assessment** is a specialist marketing agency focused on fraud prevention and compliance solutions for UK organizations. We help businesses navigate complex regulatory requirements through innovative technology and expert guidance.

### Brand Mission
To empower UK organizations with cutting-edge fraud risk assessment tools that ensure compliance, protect assets, and build resilient fraud prevention frameworks.

### Brand Values
- **Compliance Excellence** - Meeting and exceeding regulatory standards
- **User-Centric Design** - Accessible, intuitive interfaces for all users
- **Data Security** - Enterprise-grade protection of sensitive information
- **Innovation** - Leveraging AI and automation for better outcomes
- **Transparency** - Clear reporting and actionable insights

## Brand Identity

### Primary Colors
Based on UK Government Design System:
- **Primary Blue:** #1d70b8 (Trust, professionalism)
- **Dark Blue:** #003078 (Authority, security)
- **Light Blue:** #5694ca (Accessibility, clarity)
- **Success Green:** #00703c (Compliance, achievement)
- **Warning Orange:** #f47738 (Attention, caution)
- **Error Red:** #d4351c (Risk, alerts)

- **Headings:** System font stack (platform-optimized)

### Brand Voice
- Professional yet approachable

- Transparent about capabilities

## Target Audience

### Primary Markets
1. **UK SMEs (50-250 employees)**
   - Need: Basic compliance framework
   - Package: Health Check or With Awareness
   - Decision maker: Finance Director, Risk Manager

2. **UK Mid-Market (250-1000 employees)**
   - Need: Comprehensive fraud prevention program
   - Package: With Dashboard
   - Decision maker: CFO, Compliance Officer

3. **UK Large Enterprises (1000+ employees)**
   - Need: Enterprise-wide risk management
   - Package: Custom enterprise solution
   - Decision maker: Chief Risk Officer, Board level

### Buyer Personas

#### Persona 1: Compliance Charlie
- **Role:** Compliance Officer
- **Pain Points:** Complex regulations, limited resources, reporting burden
- **Motivations:** Avoid penalties, streamline compliance, prove due diligence
- **Message:** "Automate your fraud risk compliance and focus on strategic initiatives"

#### Persona 2: Risk-Aware Rachel
- **Role:** Risk Manager
- **Pain Points:** Fragmented risk data, reactive approach, lack of visibility
- **Motivations:** Proactive risk management, data-driven decisions, board confidence
- **Message:** "Transform fraud risk from a compliance burden to a strategic advantage"

#### Persona 3: Finance Director Frank
- **Role:** Finance Director
- **Pain Points:** Fraud losses, audit findings, regulatory pressure
- **Motivations:** Protect assets, reduce losses, demonstrate governance
- **Message:** "Quantify, prioritize, and mitigate fraud risks with confidence"
